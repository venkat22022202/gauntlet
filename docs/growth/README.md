Research commissioned to answer: *why isn't Gauntlet getting traction, and what bold,
concrete moves would make it solve a real 2026 problem, look unmistakable, and get
talked about?*

Three parallel research streams + a product audit fed into one conclusion.

visual identity**. Details in `00-STRATEGY.md`.
